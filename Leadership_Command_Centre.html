Customer value tiers

How the value score is calculated

FactorWeightHighLow

Gross profit enters as a log-scaled magnitude — bigger euros score higher, so large accounts pull well ahead of small ones. Frequency, momentum and own-driver are the account’s own figures. Points = weight × factor; the four add up to the 0–100 score. Tiers: A 70+, B 55–69, C 35–54, D under 35.
